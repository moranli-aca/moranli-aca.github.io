TextSculptor is a comprehensive framework for data construction and evaluation in scene text editing. It introduces **TextSculpt-Data**, a dataset with 3.2 million training samples: 1.2 million OCR-verified text-to-image samples and 2 million paired text-editing samples with aligned source and target images.

The work also presents **TextSculpt-Bench**, covering text addition, replacement, removal, and hybrid editing. Its evaluation protocol jointly measures text accuracy, visual quality, and background preservation, providing a standardized way to assess scene-text editing systems.
