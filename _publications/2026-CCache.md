Wenxiao Wu, Yachen Gao, Yukang Feng, Chengming Xu, **Moran Li**, Jingyu Li, Ming Xie, Xiaobin Hu, Xinwei Sun, Jing-Hao Xue, Nong Sang, Yanwei Fu<br>

Conformal Cache develops a reliable caching framework for fast generative inference. It calibrates the discrepancy between lightweight proxy signals and full-model computation, enabling efficient cache reuse while controlling approximation risk.
